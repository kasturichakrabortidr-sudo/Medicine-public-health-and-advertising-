# Visuals skill

The picture is the argument. Copy captions it.

## What owns a beat

- Clinic of 100 (one paper) for the prize
- Forest for agreement
- Compare bars for a second paper’s job
- Spine (two rows) for paper → move
- Board of 2–5 cards for jobs, pillars, objections, audiences, PICO
- Two-stat for asked vs need, and current vs required
- Flow for sequence and the 30-day ask

Never stack a chart, a table, and bullets. People-grid: one paper.
Spine: at most two rows. Numbers only from numbered papers. INN, not brand.

## After a change

A content slide without `chart`, `board`, `flow`, or `stat` is a craft failure
unless it is the Vancouver list.
