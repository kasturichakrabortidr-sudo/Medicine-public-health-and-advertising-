# Copy skill

Titles are conclusions. They are complete. A hanging “if”, “at”, or “the”
is a defect. An ellipsis is a defect.

## Rules

1. Author a short complete line. Do not crop the working-file paragraph.
2. If a sourced claim is long, take the first full sentence. Stop at `. ? !`
3. If a phrase has no stop, keep it only when it is already a whole clause,
   then end it with a period. Do not add `…`
4. Title: a complete sentence or a complete noun phrase, at most 12 words
5. Narrative: at most two complete sentences
6. Card body: one complete sentence
7. Science uses the INN. Do not invent numbers

## After a change

No slide title, subtitle, narrative, card, or flow line may contain `…`
or end on a hanging function word.
